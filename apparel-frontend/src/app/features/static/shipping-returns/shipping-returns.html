Orders & Fulfillment

Shipping & Returns Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Shipping Policy

  • Processing Time: Orders are processed and dispatched within 1 to 2 business days (excluding Sundays and national holidays).
  • Delivery Timelines: Metro cities typically receive orders within 3–5 business days. Tier-2/Tier-3 cities and regional areas take approximately 5–7 business days.
  • Shipping Charges: Standard shipping charges are calculated during checkout based on delivery zone. Free shipping promotions apply to qualifying cart minimums.
  • Order Tracking: Once dispatched, live shipment tracking updates and SMS/Email notifications are sent immediately.

Returns & Exchange Policy

We want you to love your purchase. If a garment doesn't fit properly or arrives damaged, we offer an easy return and exchange process:

  • Return Window: Return or exchange requests must be initiated within 7 days of delivery through your My Orders account dashboard.
  • Condition Requirements: Items must be unworn, unwashed, unused, and returned in their original packaging with all brand tags intact.
  • Refund Turnaround: Once our warehouse inspects and approves the returned item, prepaid refunds (Razorpay/Cards/UPI) are credited to your original payment source within 5–7 business days.

Order Cancellation

Orders can be cancelled directly from your account before they enter the SHIPPED stage. Once dispatched, cancellations cannot be processed; please initiate a standard return upon delivery instead.

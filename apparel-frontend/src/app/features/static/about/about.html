About Ladies Apparel

Rooted in Jaipur’s rich textile heritage, Ladies Apparel was founded to bring together timeless Indian craftsmanship and contemporary everyday wear.

From handcrafted Chikankari tunics and regal Banarasi silk weaves to breathable everyday cotton kurtis and modern western maxis, our collections celebrate the vibrancy, grace, and individuality of every woman.

We prioritize fabric breathability, long-lasting dyes, comfortable silhouettes, and transparent customer service. Whether dressing for festivities or everyday work, you deserve garments that feel as good as they look.

Security & Data

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, browse products, or make a purchase at apparel.bhawesh.shop, we collect necessary personal information including your full name, email address, contact number, delivery addresses, and encrypted authentication credentials.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to:

  • Process and deliver orders to your specified destination.
  • Send transactional emails, OTP verification codes, and order tracking notifications.
  • Prevent fraudulent transactions and secure user accounts.

3. Payment Security

We do not store your credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or UPI PINs on our servers. All financial transactions are securely tokenized and handled through RBI-compliant, PCI-DSS certified payment gateways (e.g. Razorpay).

4. Third-Party Disclosures

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared solely with vetted courier partners (for order delivery) and cloud messaging infrastructure (for OTP/order status alerts).

5. Contact Our Privacy Officer

For any questions regarding your personal data or to request account deletion, please email us at apparel@bhawesh.shop.
